ReshapedPDF

Privacy

The short version, because there is not much to say.

ReshapedPDF has no servers. There is no account to create, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no analytics, and no upload. Your documents are opened, edited and saved on your own machine. We could not read them if we wanted to, because they are never sent anywhere.

The desktop app

Everything happens locally. The app makes no network request of its own — not on launch, not on export, not ever — with the three exceptions below, each of which you start deliberately.

The web app

The browser version is the same code, served as static files. Your PDF is read by the browser itself and never leaves the tab; there is no upload endpoint to send it to. Closing the tab discards everything.

The three times anything leaves your machine

  • An AI model you connect. The AI tools are bring-your-own-model. If you point the app at a local model (Ollama, LM Studio) nothing leaves your machine at all. If you point it at a hosted API with your own key, then the crop of the page you are working on is sent to that provider, under your agreement with them — never through us. The endpoint is whatever you typed in, and you can see it in the AI panel at any time.
  • Checking for a new version, if you ask it to. Release downloads are hosted on GitHub; opening that page is an ordinary visit to GitHub and subject to their privacy policy.
  • The list of models, when you open the Ollama setup panel. It fetches the public catalogue of vision models from ollama.com so it can show you what is worth installing and how big each one is. No part of your document is involved — it is the same page anyone can open in a browser — and the panel says live from ollama.com above the list, so it is never happening invisibly. The result is cached on your device for a day. Pick any other provider and this request is not made at all.

What is stored on your device

Local browser/app storage keeps only your own preferences: the theme, your saved signature, the AI endpoint and model name you entered, and the cached Ollama model list. An API key you type is kept on your device so you do not have to retype it. None of it is transmitted. Clearing site data (web) or deleting the app's support folder (desktop) removes all of it.

This website

Static pages. No cookies, no trackers, no analytics scripts, no embedded third parties — the typefaces are served from this domain rather than from a font CDN, precisely so that visiting this page does not tell anyone else that you did. Your host will keep ordinary server logs.

Your rights

Since no personal data is collected, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete. If you believe otherwise, or you have any question about the above, write to anton.arnaudov.pro@gmail.com and you will get a real answer.
